Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what data the CTRL TOUCH website and CTRL TOUCH Studio handle, and why. Short version: we only use the data needed to upload our videos to TikTok, it stays on our own computer, and we never sell or share it.

1. This website

This website has no account, no form, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and secure the service.

2. Data used by CTRL TOUCH Studio

When a TikTok account is connected through TikTok's official login page, the tool receives and uses:

  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so the tool can act on the account after the owner's approval;
  • The account's open ID and granted permissions (for example user.info.basic and video.upload);
  • Basic profile and posting settings returned by TikTok (display name, username, available privacy options, maximum video length), shown before each upload;
  • The videos and captions the account owner chooses to send.

We never see or store the TikTok password: sign-in happens on TikTok's own page.

3. How we use it

Only to send the chosen videos to TikTok, as drafts or as posts confirmed by the account owner, and to check their processing status. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored in a private file on the CTRL TOUCH computer that runs the tool. They are not uploaded to any server other than TikTok's, and they are never shared, sold or rented to anyone.

5. Sharing

Videos, captions and settings are sent only to TikTok, through its official API, and only after a manual confirmation. TikTok handles them under its own Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account is connected. Disconnecting in the tool revokes the access with TikTok and deletes the local tokens. You can also remove access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). Refresh tokens expire automatically after the period set by TikTok.

7. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to ctrltouch95@gmail.com.

8. Children

This website and the tool are not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
